🚀 Major Launch: Massive Updates to the Hidalgo Dems Digital Infrastructure

Team,

Over the past week, we’ve been working aggressively behind the scenes to modernize the Hidalgo County Democratic Party’s digital infrastructure. Our goal was simple: we want our local tech and voter resources to match—and exceed—the operational standards of the best-funded, top-tier counties in Texas.

Today, those major updates are officially live at HidalgoCountyDems.org.

We have launched nearly a dozen brand-new strategic features designed to streamline our operations, recruit better talent, and protect our voters.

Here is what you need to know about the new tools at your disposal:

🛡️ Protecting & Empowering Voters

  • Voter Protection Hub: We’ve built a dedicated emergency portal for Election Day. If a voter experiences machine failures or intimidation, they can instantly connect to the 1-844-TX-VOTES boiler room with a single tap. It also includes our alternate language and ADA hotlines, plus a "Know Your Rights" checklist.
  • Vote By Mail Funnel: A new, streamlined, highly accessible guide explicitly breaking down the complex eligibility requirements for Mail-In ballots and linking the exact PDFs voters need.
  • Global Accessibility Widget: Our entire website now features an interactive ADA widget in the corner of every screen, allowing older and visually impaired voters to instantly switch on High Contrast mode, enlarge text, or disable animations.
  • Ballot Board Explainer: A visual guide breaking down the exact internal process for the Central Counting Station regarding early voting and provisional ballots.

⚙️ Organizer & Precinct Tools

  • Precinct Chair Database Updates: As requested yesterday! The Precinct Chair roster is fully updated with all incoming contacts and includes the updated CD 15 and CD 28 Congressional Maps, automatically tracking "Split District" parameters for immediate visibility based on our new data.
  • VAN Tech Support Portal: Precinct Chairs & Campaigns! Instead of playing text-message telephone for database issues, we now have a formal tech ticketing system. If you need help with MiniVAN, password resets, or list cutting, you can submit a direct ticket through the website under the Action ▼ tab!
  • Unified Events Calendar: A single, central calendar to track all official party activities, block walks, and local rallies.

🤝 Recruitment & Transparency

  • Work With Us (Internships): We are rebranding volunteering for young people into structured Data, Analytics, and Organizing Fellowships to vastly improve college student recruitment.
  • Election Worker Recruitment: A direct pipeline pushing interested Democrats straight into the Election Clerk/Judge pipeline to defend our local polling locations.
  • Run For Office Guide: A candidate filing portal explaining exactly how local activists can get their name on the ballot for municipal and county elections.
  • Press & Media Hub: A localized blog and press release archive where we will publish our official statements and rapid-response commentary.
