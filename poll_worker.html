❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do poll workers get paid for attending mandatory training?
Yes! The Hidalgo County Elections Department pays poll workers for attending required training sessions prior to Election Day.
Q: What is the difference between an Election Judge and an Election Clerk?
Election Judges supervise the polling place, ensure compliance with Texas Election Code, and oversee ballot security. Election Clerks assist voters with check-in, verify voter IDs, and issue ballots.
Q: What if I am not currently registered to vote in Hidalgo County?
You must be a registered voter to serve as a poll worker. You can check your registration status or register to vote through our Voter Resources Page before applying.
Q: Can high school students serve as poll workers?
Yes! Eligible high school students aged 16 or older who meet student election clerk criteria can apply with school and parental permission.
