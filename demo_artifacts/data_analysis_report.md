# Precinct 107 Early Voting Analysis Report

**Prepared By:** Data Analyst

**Subject:** Explosive Youth Voter Turnout in Precinct 107

## Executive Summary
A massive anomaly in early voting turnout has been detected in Precinct 107. Compared to historical baselines from the 2022 midterms, early voting among the 18-29 demographic has surged by over 400%.

- **2026 Total Youth Votes (EV Day 1-3):** 732

3. **Peer-to-Peer Relational Organizing:** The new relational organizing hub has resulted in an average of 4.2 secondary voter contacts per primary organizer in this precinct.
