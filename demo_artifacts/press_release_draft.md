**MASSIVE YOUTH VOTER SURGE IN HIDALGO COUNTY: PRECINCT 107 SEES 400% INCREASE IN EARLY VOTING**

**EDINBURG, TX —** In a stunning display of civic engagement and political power, the young voters of Hidalgo County are turning out in unprecedented numbers. Early voting data from Precinct 107 indicates a massive youth voter surge, representing an over 400% increase compared to the same period in 2022.

This historic turnout is a direct testament to the energy, passion, and determination of our community's young people who are demanding a seat at the table. They are tired of the status quo and are marching to the polls to fight for their futures, their rights, and their families.

"This isn't just an increase; it's an electoral earthquake," said the HCDP Chair. "The youth of South Texas are sending a clear, undeniable message. To anyone who doubted the power of the young vote: look at Precinct 107. The wave isn't coming—it's already here."

The Hidalgo County Democratic Party is immediately deploying additional volunteers to assist voters at Precinct 107 and neighboring sites, ensuring every single voice is heard.

We call on all residents to join this historic movement. Let's make this the highest turnout election in our county's history.
