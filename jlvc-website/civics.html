Civics 101

The Three Branches

A quick overview of how the local and federal government structures work together. Legislative creates laws, Executive enforces them, and Judicial interprets them. In Texas, our local county judges hold significant executive power.

Electoral College vs Popular Vote

While national elections use the Electoral College, every local election in the RGV is decided strictly by the popular vote. Your single vote has direct mathematical impact locally.

Civics 102

County Governance

The Commissioner's Court handles the county budget, road maintenance, and emergency services. This is why down-ballot voting is critical for your immediate neighborhood's infrastructure.

School Boards

School board trustees dictate the curriculum, budget, and policies of independent school districts (ISDs). These are non-partisan races that usually have very low turnout.
