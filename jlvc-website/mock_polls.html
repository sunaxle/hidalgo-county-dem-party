The Cameron County Model

Modeled after the highly successful initiative by the Cameron County Elections Department, the JLVC is advocating for off-cycle Mock Poll Sites in Hidalgo County.

What is a Mock Poll Site?

A fully functional simulation of an official polling location set up during non-election periods. It features actual voting machines, standard check-in procedures, and election workers.

Why It Works:

  • Demystifies the Process: Allows first-time voters and recently registered citizens to practice checking in and using the machines without the pressure of a real line.
  • Accessibility Testing: Provides disabled voters a chance to test curbside and machine accessibility features in a relaxed environment.
  • Community Trust: Builds transparency between the Elections Department and skeptical voters by demonstrating how ballots are handled and secured.
