The Power of Your Vote

In South Texas, every single vote carries immense weight. Local elections are often decided by just a handful of votes, meaning your voice directly dictates the funding of our schools, the quality of our infrastructure, and the rights of our community.

When we don't vote, we surrender our power. Registering to vote and showing up to the polls is how we honor the legacy of civil rights leaders like John Lewis and build a Rio Grande Valley that truly represents us all.

About the Coalition

Who We Are

The John Lewis Voter Coalition is a grassroots civic engagement initiative based in the Rio Grande Valley. Operating out of Hidalgo County, our coalition is dedicated to continuing the legacy of the late civil rights icon John Lewis by fighting for equitable access to the ballot box for all South Texans.

Our Mission

We believe that the right to vote is the most powerful non-violent tool we have in a democratic society. In a region as diverse and rapidly growing as the RGV, our mission is to break down barriers to voting, educate our community on their electoral rights, and mobilize historically underrepresented voices.

What We Do

  • Voter Registration & Education Helping residents navigate the registration process, understand local voting laws, and find their polling locations.
  • Community Mobilization Partnering with local organizers to get out the vote (GOTV) and ensure South Texas turnout reflects the true demographics of our community.
  • Advocacy Standing against voter suppression tactics and supporting the principles of the John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act at the local level.

A Cooperative Approach

We don't want to reinvent the wheel. We exist to fill the gaps, offering parallel services that amplify the work of existing organizations.

How We Grow Together

1

Identify Gaps

We look for areas in voter outreach and education that are currently underserved by traditional party infrastructure.

2

Provide Parallel Services

We build tools, guides, and volunteer networks that run alongside existing efforts, rather than competing for the same resources.

3

Share Data & Success

By cooperating with local non-profits and community leaders, a rising tide lifts all ships. Our voter guides and tools are open for all to share.
