Message from the Precinct Chair

★ Official Welcome • Precinct 93

“Precinct 93: Community First • Organizing Mission • Protecting Our Vote”

Dear Neighbors, Friends, and Families of Precinct 93 in Mission,

Welcome to our official precinct portal! As your Democratic Precinct Chair, I am deeply committed to serving our Mission community, making sure your voices are heard, and ensuring every single neighbor has access to the ballot box.

Our primary designated voting location for Precinct 93 is the Veterans Memorial Pavilion (300 S. Inspiration Road, Mission, TX). Whether during Early Voting or on Election Day, making a plan to vote is the most powerful tool we have to protect our public schools, healthcare, and working families.

Grassroots democracy thrives when we stay connected. That is why we have launched the Mission Precinct 93 Info WhatsApp community group, where we share verified voting updates, important community meetings, election deadlines, and local resources directly with you.

Whether you need to register to vote, check your registration status, request a free Democratic yard sign, or arrange a free ride to the Veterans Memorial Pavilion for you or an elderly family member, our team is right here to help.

Please feel free to call me directly at (956) 957-8095 or join our WhatsApp community group below. Together, we are building a stronger, more informed, and united Mission!

Precinct 93: Community First • Protecting Our Vote.
Working hand-in-hand for our families and our future.
