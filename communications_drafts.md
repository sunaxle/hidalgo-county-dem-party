## Draft 2: Focus, Transparency & Unity (Internal Update)
**To:** Precinct Chairs & Party Leadership
**From:** Chairman Richard Gonzales
**Subject:** Staying focused on the mission ahead

Team,

I want to take a moment to address our precinct chairs and core leadership. As your Chairman, my priority has always been, and will always be, electing Democrats up and down the ballot in Hidalgo County. 

I know there have been internal conversations and disagreements recently regarding party administration, specific consultant expenditures, and hiring decisions. The democratic process is built on passionate debate, and I welcome accountability and open dialogue from all precinct chairs, including those who have recently raised concerns. We are currently implementing comprehensive reviews of our operational and financial processes to ensure complete transparency, accountability, and alignment with our party's bylaws.

However, we cannot lose sight of the bigger picture. Our primary turnout has *doubled*. The DLCC is officially targeting 12 state House seats, and the Secretary of State is stepping down. Meanwhile, our public schools are facing immense financial pressures from Austin's policies, making the fight for House District 41 more urgent than ever.

We cannot afford to let internal divisions distract us from our true opponents. I am asking all of us to come together, focus our energy on turning out voters, and work as one united Hidalgo County Democratic Party. Let's meet at the table, hash out our differences constructively, and get back to the work of winning elections.

In solidarity,

**Richard Gonzales**
Chairman, Hidalgo County Democratic Party
