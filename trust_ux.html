How to Verify Our Communications

Check the Domain Name

All official web links will begin with exactly https://hidalgocountydems.org. Watch out for slight misspellings (like ending in .com or having extra/missing letters).

Official Email Sender

Our emails will always come from an address ending in @hidalgocountydems.org. We will never ask for your passwords, full SSN, or wire transfers via email.

Spotting AI-Generated Misinformation

AI audio and video can be highly convincing. If a recording of a candidate or party official sounds out of character or makes sensational claims, pause before sharing. Here is how to cross-check:

  • Verify the news on our official website or established local news outlets.
  • Look for official press releases matching the claim in our Press section.
  • Check the official, verified social media accounts of the candidate involved.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you receive a text, email, or see a website claiming to be the Hidalgo County Democratic Party that seems suspicious, please do not click any links or provide personal information. Instead, report it to our team immediately at security@hidalgocountydems.org.
