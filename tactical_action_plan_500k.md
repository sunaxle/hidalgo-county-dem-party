# Tactical Action Plan: The Road to 500k Registered Voters

## 1. Executive Summary
**Current Registration:** 460,407
**Baseline Goal:** 480,000
**Stretch Goal (BHAG):** 500,000 by October
**The Gap:** ~35,000 net new registrations (accounting for 5k natural growth).
**Timeline:** June - October (approx. 4 months)

To hit the historic half-million mark in Hidalgo County, we cannot rely on organic growth. We need an aggressive, metric-driven mobilization strategy that turns our Precinct Chairs into field commanders and our volunteers into a highly targeted registration force. This plan breaks down the exact tactics across email, phone, and block walking to achieve our 500k goal.

---

## 2. Mobilizing Precinct Chairs (The Core Engine)
Our Precinct Chairs are the ground commanders. We need to move them from "attending meetings" to "executing turf plans."

*   **Micro-Targeting:** Divide the 35,000 goal across active precincts. If we have 150 active Precinct Chairs, each chair is responsible for roughly **235 new registrations** in their turf by October (about 15 per week).
*   **The "Half-Million Club" Toolkit:** Every Chair receives a physical and digital toolkit containing:
    *   Lists of households with unregistered adults (using VAN/Votebuilder data).
    *   Voter registration forms (VRs), clipboards, and pens.
    *   Scripts for door-knocking and community events.
*   **Accountability & Gamification:** Create a public leaderboard at county meetings and via email. Recognize top-performing Precinct Chairs monthly.

---

## 3. Email Campaign Strategy
The email program will serve as the recruitment engine for volunteers and a low-friction way to encourage peer-to-peer registration.

### **Phase 1: The Kickoff (June)**
*   **Theme:** "The Road to 500k: Making Hidalgo County History"
*   **Call to Action (CTA):** Pledge to register 5 friends or family members. Sign up to be a Volunteer Deputy Registrar (VDR).

### **Phase 2: The Volunteer Ask (July - August)**
*   **Theme:** "We can't do this from the couch."
*   **Cadence:** Weekly emails targeting distinct geographic areas (e.g., "McAllen: We need you this Saturday").
*   **CTA:** RSVP for upcoming Phone Banks and Block Walks.

### **Phase 3: Progress & Urgency (September)**
*   **Theme:** "The Clock is Ticking / Leaderboard Updates"
*   **Content:** Highlight Precinct Chairs who have hit their numbers. Show a visual thermometer of our progress (e.g., "We are at 482,000... just 18k to go!").
*   **CTA:** Last-chance VDR training, final weekend pushes.

### **Phase 4: The Final Push (October)**
*   **Theme:** "The Final Deadline"
*   **Content:** Daily emails in the final 5 days before the voter registration deadline.
*   **CTA:** Forward this email to 3 unregistered people right now. Where to drop off forms.

---

## 4. Phone Banking Strategy
Phone banking will target the lowest-hanging fruit: unregistered family members in known Democratic households.

*   **The Target Audience:** "Unregistered household members." We will pull lists of strong Democratic voters who live with individuals over 18 who are not registered.
*   **The Script Angle:** *“Hi [Name], thanks for being a consistent voter. We noticed there might be someone in your household who isn't registered yet. Can we mail you a pre-stamped registration form for them, or help them register online right now?”*
*   **Execution:**
    *   **"Dialing for Democracy" Thursdays:** Weekly virtual phone banks over Zoom. Volunteers can dial from home using ActionNetwork/VAN virtual phone banks.
    *   **Pizza & Politics:** In-person phone bank parties hosted by Precinct Chairs at local libraries, party headquarters, or community centers twice a month.

---

## 5. Block Walking & Ground Strategy
Traditional block walking is essential, but we must also deploy "Hotspot" registration to hit high volume quickly.

### **Targeted Turf Walking (Saturdays)**
*   **Focus:** Walking specifically to doors of unregistered voters, not general canvassing. 
*   **Tactic:** Precinct Chairs lead small teams (2-3 volunteers) in their own neighborhoods. 
*   **Leave-Behinds:** If no one is home, leave a door hanger with a QR code linking to the Texas Secretary of State voter registration request page.

### **High-Density "Hotspot" Registration**
To get 35,000 registrations, we need bulk events.
*   **Back-to-School Campus Push (August/September):** Heavy presence at UTRGV and South Texas College (STC) campuses during the first weeks of class. Target incoming freshmen who just turned 18.
*   **Community Hubs (Weekends):** Deploy VDRs to high-traffic areas:
    *   Las Pulgas (Flea markets like Alamo, Mercedes, and Donna)
    *   Plazas and public parks on Sunday evenings.
    *   Local festivals, concerts, and high school football games.
