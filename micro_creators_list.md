# Rio Grande Valley & Hidalgo County Micro-Creators

This document tracks local community leaders, food reviewers, and culture accounts across the RGV (particularly Hidalgo County) for the Micro-Creator Media Offensive track.

## Notable Local Creators (Lifestyle & Community)
* **Kimberly Meza (@yosoykimberlymeza)**: A well-known weather presenter for Univisión who regularly shares content and updates highly relevant to the RGV community.
* **Ashley M. Gonzalez (@mrsashleygonzalez)**: An active creator in the McAllen area known for lifestyle and family-oriented content.
* **Alejandra Estrada (@alestrrada)**: A prominent content creator frequently featuring fashion, lifestyle, and local spots.
* **Alexandra Castellanos (@alexandracaste)**: A creator with a significant following based in the McAllen area.
* **Doña Anita (@donaanita77)**: Occasionally featured in local media as a recognizable and humorous local personality on TikTok.

## Food Reviewers & Culinary Accounts
* **Ivanna Mendoza / RGV Taste (@rgvtaste_)**: One of the most notable food content creators in the RGV. She focuses on showcasing local businesses, hidden gems, and the vibrant food culture of the region across Instagram and TikTok.
* **Isaac Anzaldua**: A dietician and local influencer who often discusses the Rio Grande Valley’s unique food culture and culinary habits from a health and lifestyle perspective.
* **Made in the 956**: Often featured on local news segments (such as KRGV), this series acts as an aggregator highlighting up-and-coming chefs and food creators making an impact in the region.

## Culture, News, & Grassroots Hubs
* **Trucha RGV (@truchargv on IG / @trucha_rgv on TikTok)**: A prominent grassroots multimedia platform. They focus on uplifting local stories, social movements, and the unique culture of the region to shift mainstream narratives. Essential for community alignment.
* **The Rio Grande Podcast**: A podcast dedicated to the RGV, highlighting local businesses, health, and community figures. Great for longer-form local narratives.
* **ElValleYa (@elvalleya)**: A highly active account focused on community news, memes, and daily happenings within the Rio Grande Valley.
* **Wild 104 (@wild104kbfm)**: The Valley's local radio station, which functions as a major influencer and cultural touchpoint in the local entertainment and music scene.
