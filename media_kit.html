2. Party Leadership

Richard Gonzales, County Chairman

Richard Gonzales serves as the County Chair for the Hidalgo County Democratic Party. Under his leadership, the party has seen a massive surge in primary turnout, doubling the figures from 2024. He is committed to electing Democrats up and down the ballot, protecting public education, and ensuring transparency and unity within the party's grassroots infrastructure.
