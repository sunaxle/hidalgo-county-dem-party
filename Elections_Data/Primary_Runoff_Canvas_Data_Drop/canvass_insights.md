# Hidalgo County Primary Runoff Canvas Analysis

## 1. Voting Methods Breakdown (Democratic Primary)
- **Absentee / Mail-in:** 1332 (13.7%)
- **Early Voting:** 5501 (56.7%)
- **Election Day:** 2873 (29.6%)

> **Insight:** Early Voting is the dominant method. Resource allocation for GOTV efforts should heavily front-load during the Early Voting period rather than Election Day.

## 2. Lieutenant Governor Race Analysis

- **Undervotes (Ballot Drop-off):** 571 (5.9% of total ballots)

## 3. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Capitalize on Early Voting:** With 56.7% of the Democratic base voting early and only 29.6% waiting until Election Day, campaign dollars and volunteer shifts should be massively front-loaded. "Election Day" is effectively a multi-week period; waiting until the final weekend to knock doors misses over half the electorate.
2. **Address Ballot Roll-Off:** The Lt. Governor race saw a 5.9% undervote rate (ballot drop-off). The party needs stronger slate messaging to ensure voters complete their entire ballot rather than just voting at the top of the ticket.
3. **Precinct Targeting:** Marcos Isaias Velez performed exceptionally well in Precincts 199, 105, and 054. The party should cross-reference these precincts with the CRM "P192 Non Voters" to identify high-propensity areas where ground-game infrastructure is already strong, and focus voter registration efforts there.
