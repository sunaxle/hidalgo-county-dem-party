# Press Releases

## 2026 Primary Turnout and Party Unity

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
**Date:** June 8, 2026
**Contact:** info@hidalgocountydems.org

**Hidalgo County Democratic Party Celebrates Historic Voter Turnout Rebound in 2026 Primary; Chairman Richard Gonzales Emphasizes Transparency and Unity Ahead of Midterms**

EDINBURG, TX — The Hidalgo County Democratic Party (HCDP) today highlighted a massive surge in voter enthusiasm, celebrating a remarkable milestone: Democratic turnout in the Rio Grande Valley more than doubled during the 2026 primary elections compared to 2024. This significant rebound across Hidalgo, Cameron, Starr, and Willacy counties reflects the deep-rooted commitment of local voters to democratic values and the tireless grassroots efforts of precinct chairs and volunteers.

Chairman Richard Gonzales praised the record-breaking engagement, calling it a testament to the community's resilience and determination. "The voters of the Rio Grande Valley have spoken loudly and clearly. They are demanding representation that fights for their families, their healthcare, and their futures. We are incredibly proud of this historic turnout, and we will carry this momentum directly into the November general election," said Chairman Gonzales.

As the Party gears up for the 2026 cycle, a major focus will be retaining Texas House District 41, following the announcement that longtime incumbent Representative Bobby Guerra will not seek re-election. The HCDP extends its deepest gratitude to Rep. Guerra for his years of dedicated service to Hidalgo County. The Party is fully mobilized and working diligently to ensure a strong Democratic successor is elected to champion the needs of the district.

In response to recent internal discussions regarding party operations, Chairman Gonzales reaffirmed his unwavering commitment to financial transparency, accountability, and collaborative leadership. The Party recognizes the passionate involvement of its precinct chairs and remains dedicated to addressing their concerns constructively.

"Our strength lies in our unity and our shared vision for a prosperous Hidalgo County," Gonzales added. "We welcome robust dialogue and are taking proactive steps to ensure our operations are fully transparent and inclusive. By working together—precinct chairs, volunteers, and staff—we are building an unstoppable infrastructure to deliver victories for Democrats up and down the ballot."

Looking ahead to the midterms, the HCDP will continue to expand its volunteer network, execute massive block-walking and phone-banking campaigns, and register thousands of new voters. With National Democrats increasingly targeting Republican-held seats in the Texas House, Hidalgo County stands ready to be a critical battleground for turning Texas blue.

For more information, to request VAN access, or to volunteer with the Hidalgo County Democratic Party, please visit hidalgocountydems.org or contact info@hidalgocountydems.org.
